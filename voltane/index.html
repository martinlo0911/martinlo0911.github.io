FAQ

Where is my data stored? Only on your iPhone. We don't run servers and can't see your cars or records.

Why does my fuel economy show "–"? It is measured between two Full Tank fill-ups of the same car. Make sure both are marked Full Tank, not Partial Fill. Electric cars: between two charges to the same Charge Limit.

Can I use miles, litres or my own currency? Yes. Choose miles or kilometres, gallons or litres, and the fuel economy style in the side menu, under Units. Each car keeps its own currency.

How do I back up, or move to a new phone? Side menu › iCloud Backup (Voltane Pro). Back up on the old phone, then restore the backup on the new one.

How do I restore my purchase? Side menu › Voltane Pro › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I remove ads? Upgrade to Voltane Pro — side menu › Voltane Pro.

How do I change the language? Side menu › Language opens Voltane's page in iPhone Settings, where you pick the language. Voltane reopens in the new language.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

Developer: ZHAOYANG LU · Contact: martinlo0911@yahoo.com

This Privacy Policy explains how the Voltane app ("Voltane", "the app", "we", "us") handles your information.

The short version

  • Your cars and records stay on your device. We do not run servers, and we cannot see your vehicles, fill-ups, charges, prices or stations.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect information from your device to show and measure ads. You decide whether ads can be personalised.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information you enter into the app

Everything you record in Voltane, including your vehicles and their photos, fill-ups, charges, odometer readings, prices, stations and favourite stations, is stored only on your device.

We do not have access to this information, and it is never sent to us.

iCloud Backup (optional)

If you use iCloud Backup in the app, copies of your data are saved in your own private iCloud storage, using your Apple Account. This storage is managed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot access your iCloud backups.

Location (optional)

If you allow it, the app uses your location while you use it, to show gas stations or EV chargers near you on the Stations tab. The search is made through Apple Maps, under Apple's Privacy Policy. Your location is not stored by the app and is never sent to us. Voltane works without location access.

Photo library

The app opens your photo library only when you choose a photo for a vehicle. The photo you pick is stored in the app on your device and is not sent to us.

Import and export

When you import or export a file (such as a CSV or spreadsheet), the file is processed on your device. Where the exported file goes is up to you (for example, you might share it by email or save it to Files).

Hakobox (optional)

If you also use Hakobox, our budgeting app, and turn on "Add to Hakobox", Voltane passes the fill-ups and charges you choose to Hakobox through a shared space on your device. Nothing leaves your device for this.

2. Advertising (free version)

The free version of Voltane shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The paid version does not show ads.

To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see below);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (for example, device model, operating system version and language);
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps).

Google does not receive your vehicles or records from Voltane. We do not share your fill-ups, charges, prices or locations with Google or any advertiser.

How Google uses this information is described in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices about ads

  • App Tracking Transparency (all regions): iOS asks whether Voltane may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: you will be asked for your consent, through Google's consent message, before ads are personalised. You can change your choice later.
  • Remove ads completely: upgrade to the paid version.

3. Exchange rates

To convert a record paid in another currency, the app downloads public exchange rates from a third-party service (api.frankfurter.dev). This request contains no personal information and none of your records. Like any internet request, it reveals your IP address to that service.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you have an active purchase, so the app can unlock paid features.

5. Children

Voltane is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because your records are stored on your device, you control them. You can delete individual records and vehicles in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. iCloud backups can be deleted from within the app, or in your iPhone's iCloud settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or the UK, or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold your app data, most of these requests can be handled directly in the app or through your device settings. For information collected by Google for advertising, see Google's Privacy Policy. You can also contact us with any request.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at martinlo0911@yahoo.com.

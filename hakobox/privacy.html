Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Developer: ZHAOYANG LU · Contact: martinlo0911@yahoo.com

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hakobox app ("Hakobox", "the app", "we", "us") handles your information.

The short version

  • Your financial records stay on your device. We do not run servers, and we cannot see your accounts, transactions, budgets or categories.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect information from your device to show and measure ads. You decide whether ads can be personalised.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information you enter into the app

Everything you record in Hakobox, including accounts, balances, transactions, categories, budgets, notes, payees, Autopay rules and any photos you attach (such as receipts or account icons), is stored only on your device.

We do not have access to this information, and it is never sent to us.

iCloud Backup (optional)

If you turn on iCloud Backup in the app, copies of your data are saved in your own private iCloud storage, using your Apple ID. This storage is managed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot access your iCloud backups.

Camera and photo library

The app asks for access to your camera or photo library only when you choose to attach a photo. Photos you pick are stored in the app on your device and are not sent to us.

Import and export

When you import or export a file (such as a CSV or spreadsheet), the file is processed on your device. Where the exported file goes is up to you (for example, you might share it by email or save it to Files).

2. Advertising (free version)

The free version of Hakobox shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The paid version does not show ads.

To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see below);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (for example, device model, operating system version and language);
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps).

Google does not receive your financial records from Hakobox. We do not share your accounts, transactions, budgets or notes with Google or any advertiser.

How Google uses this information is described in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices about ads

  • App Tracking Transparency (all regions): iOS asks whether Hakobox may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: you will be asked for your consent, through Google's consent message, before ads are personalised. You can change your choice later.
  • Remove ads completely: upgrade to the paid version.

3. Exchange rates

To convert between currencies, the app downloads public exchange rates from a third-party service (open.er-api.com). This request contains no personal or financial information. Like any internet request, it reveals your IP address to that service.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you have an active purchase, so the app can unlock paid features.

5. Children

Hakobox is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because your records are stored on your device, you control them. You can delete individual items in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. iCloud backups can be deleted from within the app, or in your iPhone's iCloud settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or the UK), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold your app data, most of these requests can be handled directly in the app or through your device settings. For information collected by Google for advertising, see Google's Privacy Policy. You can also contact us with any request.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at martinlo0911@yahoo.com.

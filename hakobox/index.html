FAQ

Where is my data stored? Only on your iPhone. We don't run servers and can't see your accounts or transactions.

How do I back up, or move to a new phone? Settings › iCloud Backup (Hakobox Pro). Back up on the old phone, then restore the backup on the new one.

How do I restore my purchase? Settings › Hakobox Pro › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I remove ads? Upgrade to Hakobox Pro — Settings › Hakobox Pro.

How do I change the language? Settings › Language opens Hakobox's page in iPhone Settings, where you pick the language. Hakobox reopens in the new language.

Can I bring in records from another app? Yes — Settings › Import & Export (Hakobox Pro) reads CSV and Excel (.xlsx) files.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my records stored?

Pet profiles, photos and care records are stored in the app on your device. No Tailnest account is required. We do not run a server that stores your pet records, so we cannot recover records you have deleted. Copies you export, share or keep through Apple’s device-backup services are handled separately.

What if my pet is not a cat or a dog?

Choose Other when selecting the animal, then enter its name or type. You can also enter a breed yourself. For cats and dogs, use Can’t find it? Type your own at the bottom of the breed list.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Check that the reminder is enabled in Tailnest, then check Settings → Notifications → Tailnest on your device. Focus settings and Scheduled Summary can affect when notifications appear. If an appointment is added to your calendar, also check its calendar alert and Calendar notification settings.

How do I export or import records?

Open Settings → Import & Export. Export records to CSV, or use the health-record PDF export on the pet ID card page. Available export periods depend on your plan. CSV does not include photo moments, attached photos or receipts. Import requires Tailnest Pro and accepts Tailnest’s CSV or Excel files; review the preview before confirming.

What does the pet ID card QR code share?

It displays the information you enable under Shown When Scanned. That information is encoded in the QR link itself. Anyone who has the link or a copy of the QR code can read the included details. Changing the switches or deleting the pet does not revoke links or screenshots already shared.

How do I update a card in Apple Wallet?

Wallet cards do not update automatically. After changing your pet’s details, open its ID card and use Add to Apple Wallet again. The new card replaces the earlier card for that pet. Apple Wallet may be unavailable on some devices or in a simulator.

Does the free version show ads?

Yes. The free version displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Tailnest Pro removes in-app ads. Personalised advertising and tracking are subject to the required consent and system permission. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I restore Tailnest Pro?

Open the Tailnest Pro purchase page from Settings and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. A lifetime purchase is a one-time purchase; a monthly subscription stays active according to its App Store subscription status.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Open your device’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tailnest. Apple handles subscription billing and cancellation. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. For a refund request, visit Apple’s Report a Problem website.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings → Language in Tailnest. This opens the app’s page in your device settings, where you can choose a supported language. The system manages the language change.

How do I remove records or shared information?

Delete records or a pet in the app. Separately remove exported files, emails, calendar events and Wallet cards where needed. Previously shared QR links cannot be remotely revoked. See the retention and deletion section of the Privacy Policy for details.

For your pet’s care

Tailnest helps you keep records and reminders. It does not diagnose conditions, recommend treatment or replace advice from a veterinarian. If your pet is unwell or needs urgent care, contact a veterinarian.

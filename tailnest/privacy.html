2. Your records and device permissions

Tailnest stores the pet and owner information you enter in the app on your device. This can include names, photos, microchip numbers, contact details, veterinary details, health and medication records, appointments, notes and receipts. We do not receive this database through a Tailnest account or server. We can receive information you separately choose to send to us, such as in a support email.

  • Camera and photos: used when you choose to add an image or save a card image. Image processing for the pet ID photo and microchip reading takes place on your device.
  • Calendar: if you enable Add to iPhone Calendar, Tailnest creates and manages the appointment you choose to add. The app does not use other calendar events for advertising.
  • Notifications: care reminders and medication notifications are scheduled locally. Delivery and visibility depend on your device settings.

You can manage permissions in your device settings. Denying a permission may limit the feature that needs it. Apple’s device backups and any calendar-account syncing you enable are governed by your Apple or calendar-provider settings and policies.

3. Pet-card QR links, Wallet and exports

The pet-card QR link contains the details you enable under Shown When Scanned. Those details are encoded after the # in the link and decoded in the visitor’s browser. The URL fragment is not included in the normal request to the website host. GitHub Pages may still process connection information when the page loads.

Anyone with the QR code or link can view the information encoded in it. A recipient may save or forward it. Changing the sharing switches, reissuing a card or deleting a pet does not change or revoke old links, printed cards or screenshots. Only include information you are comfortable sharing.

Wallet cards are generated on your device and passed to Apple Wallet at your request. They do not update automatically. Exported CSV or PDF files and shared photos are created on your device; you choose their destination through the system sharing interface. Copies held by recipients or other services are subject to those recipients’ practices.

4. Advertising and your choices

The free version of Tailnest displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Tailnest Pro removes in-app ads. We do not provide pet records, photos, owner phone numbers or veterinary details to Google for advertising.

Google’s advertising SDK may process device identifiers, approximate location (including from an IP address), ad views and taps, product interactions, crash logs and performance information. It uses this information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and technical operation. Where identifiers are involved, information can be associated with a device.

Our advertising approach is to use personalised ads and cross-app tracking only after the required consent and system permission have been obtained. If you decline tracking, Tailnest must not access the IDFA or track you across other companies’ apps and websites. Depending on your consent choices, region and ad availability, non-personalised or limited ads may be shown, or an ad may not be available.

  • Tracking permission: manage Tailnest’s permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device. If the system does not offer tracking permission, we do not treat that as consent.
  • Advertising consent: where required, Google’s consent message asks for your choices. Required privacy-options controls let you review or withdraw those choices. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that lawfully occurred before withdrawal.
  • No ads: Tailnest Pro removes in-app advertising.

Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy for Google’s processing, retention and privacy controls.

5. Other services and support

Exchange rates: Tailnest requests dates and currency codes from Frankfurter and caches rates on the device. Like other internet services, the service and its infrastructure can receive technical request information such as your IP address.

Purchases: Apple handles purchases, subscriptions and payment details. Tailnest uses purchase entitlement information to unlock features. We do not receive your payment-card or bank-account details.

Email support: contacting the developer opens your email app. An in-app support email can include the app version, device model, system version and app language. Nothing is sent until you send it. We receive your email address, message, the included technical information and any attachments you choose to provide. We use them to respond and resolve the request. Avoid sending information that is not needed for support.

Website hosting: these pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. The host may process IP addresses and other connection or request information for delivery and security. We do not add our own website advertising or analytics scripts. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

6. Retention and deletion

You control records stored in the app and can delete individual records or pets. Removing the app normally removes its local app data; offloading the app may keep that data. Exported files, sent emails, calendar entries, Wallet cards, recipient copies and backups can remain elsewhere and must be managed separately. Previously shared QR links cannot be remotely revoked.

We keep support correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to address the request, maintain relevant support records or meet legal obligations. Google, Apple, GitHub and any other service you choose apply their own retention policies to information they process.

7. Sharing, rights and security

We do not sell your pet records or use them to build advertising profiles. Information may be processed by the service providers described above, shared at your direction, or disclosed where required by law. Ad-related processing may have different legal definitions of “sharing” depending on where you live; use the available consent controls and contact us if you have a question.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, withdraw consent, or object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us at the email address above. We can address information we hold, such as support messages; we cannot retrieve a pet database we do not possess. You may also have a right to complain to your local data-protection authority. Service providers’ privacy policies explain how to exercise rights relating to their processing.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, under their applicable terms and privacy safeguards. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device, review sharing choices and avoid placing private information in a publicly shared QR code.

8. Children

Tailnest is intended for pet owners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app, our practices or applicable requirements change. The updated date appears at the top of this page.

1. 我哋唔收集你嘅個人資料

叫乜好唔使開帳戶、唔使登入。App 本身唔會收集、上載或者出售任何個人資料。

2. 存喺你部手機嘅資料

以下資料只會存喺你部 iPhone，我哋睇唔到、亦唔會傳去任何伺服器：

  • 你喺設定揀嘅嘢：性別（用嚟揀啱嘅稱呼，例如外甥定姨甥）、拼音顯示、外觀同顏色；
  • 你查過嘅記錄（最近 100 條）；
  • 廣告播放次數嘅計數。

你可以喺「記錄」頁清除記錄；刪除 app 就會連同以上所有資料一齊刪除。

3. 讀音

撳讀音掣時，app 用 iPhone 內置嘅廣東話語音喺手機上面讀出，唔會上載任何內容。

4. 廣告（Google AdMob）

免費版會顯示由 Google AdMob 提供嘅廣告。為咗顯示廣告同計算廣告成效，Google 可能會收集：

  • 裝置嘅廣告識別碼——只會喺你喺 iPhone 嘅詢問度撳「允許」追蹤之後先會用；
  • IP 地址同由此推算嘅大概位置、裝置型號、作業系統版本；
  • 你同廣告嘅互動（例如有冇撳廣告）。

你隨時可以喺 iPhone「設定」›「私隱與安全性」›「追蹤」關閉追蹤。身處歐洲經濟區、英國或瑞士嘅用家，開 app 時會見到 Google 嘅同意畫面。 Google 點樣使用呢啲資料：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

買咗「移除廣告」之後，app 唔會再載入任何廣告。

5. 購買

「移除廣告」由 Apple 嘅 App Store 處理。我哋收唔到你嘅姓名、付款資料或者 Apple 帳戶資料，只會由 App Store 知道你係咪已經購買。

6. 分享資料

除咗上面第 4 段（Google）同第 5 段（Apple）所講，我哋唔會同任何人分享或者出售資料。

7. 兒童

叫乜好唔係專為 13 歲以下兒童而設，我哋亦唔會蓄意收集兒童嘅資料。

8. 更改

9. 聯絡我哋

English

1. We don't collect your personal data

CantoFamily has no accounts and no sign-in. The app itself does not collect, upload or sell any personal data.

2. Data kept on your phone

The following stays on your iPhone only — we can't see it and it is never sent to a server:

  • your choices in Settings: sex (used to pick the right term, e.g. 外甥 or 姨甥), romanisation,

appearance and colour;

  • your lookup history (the latest 100);
  • a count of how many ads have been shown.

You can clear your history on the History tab; deleting the app deletes all of the above.

3. Pronunciation

The play button uses iPhone's built-in Cantonese voice, on the phone. Nothing is uploaded.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads and measure them, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier — **only if you tap "Allow" when iPhone asks about

tracking**;

  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device model and system version;
  • how you interact with ads (for example, whether you tap one).

You can turn tracking off at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland see Google's consent form when opening the app. How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Once you buy Remove Ads, the app loads no ads at all.

5. Purchases

Remove Ads is handled by Apple's App Store. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account details — only whether you have bought it.

6. Sharing

Apart from Google (section 4) and Apple (section 5), we don't share or sell any data.

7. Children

CantoFamily is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: martinlo0911@yahoo.com.
